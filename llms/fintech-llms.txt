# Fin

> Fin enables users and businesses to move millions of dollars instantly—whether to other Fin users, directly into bank accounts, or across crypto rails. It provides business-grade payment rails that feel like a payment app, not crypto.

Key capabilities:
- **Instant Settlement:** Global settlement in seconds, not days.
- **High Value:** Engineered for moving millions of dollars.
- **Asset Support:** Native support to deposit/withdraw to bank accounts, plus send/receive USDT, USDC, ETH, & BTC.
- **Abstraction:** Users interact with a clean fintech interface, while Fin handles the complex crypto rails in the background.
